a UX redesign you can open in a browser.

Five artifacts. One understands what's broken, one fixes the flagship problem (notes), and three offer navigation directions. Open them in order, then pick a spine. Built to the brand system — dark-first, pink→cyan, the strength / temperature / tier motifs throughout.

What we found

  • Notes live in 4 disconnected stores fed by 7 input boxes — two tiny (rows=2), one silently lost.
  • The #1 complaint: notes are "shit, very small, not accessible at a glance."
  • 3 different fan-detail renderers show the same person three ways.
  • ⌘K dead-ends in a legacy drawer — you can't reach the full profile.
  • No quick-peek: every fan click is a full-page navigation that loses your place.

What we recommend

  • Adopt one canonical notes model: a pinned About + a dated notes log in the timeline.the model
  • Make Direction B the navigation spine: ⌘K + one reusable peek everywhere.spine
  • Make Direction C the home screen: people-queues, not KPI tiles.home
  • Collapse the 3 renderers into one record at three depths: glance → focus → full page.
